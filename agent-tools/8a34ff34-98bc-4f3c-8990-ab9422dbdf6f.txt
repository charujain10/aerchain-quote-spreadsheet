# Aerchain

Aerchain is a Software Development company. Aerchain is an AI-powered procurement and autonomous sourcing company that simplifies sourcing, negotiations, and compliance, ensuring faster cycles and cost savings. Aerchain employs 123 people (+78.3% YoY, +54 people) and has an annual revenue of $500K, founded in 2019. Headquartered in Bengaluru, Karnataka, India, with presence in United States.

## About

Aerchain builds an AI-Native Spend Operating System for enterprise procurement worldwide - it doesn’t exist anywhere else yet. Our vision is to free procurement from the grunt work, sharpen the decisions that matter, and give teams the foresight to proactively capture savings and opportunities.
